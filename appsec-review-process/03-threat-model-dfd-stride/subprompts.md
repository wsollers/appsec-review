# Subprompts — Threat Model / DFD / STRIDE

## Per-Component Dataflow

For one component, enumerate inbound and outbound flows, protocol, data class, authentication, and
evidence citations.

## Cross-Environment Diagram

Collapse per-component flows into environment-level flows suitable for executive reporting.

## Data-Tag Flow Trace

Trace one data category, such as credentials, location, telemetry, payment, chat, or device ID.
