# Job correctness audit: cumulative findings

**All 47 registered jobs have completed their dedicated-agent audit.** The audit found correctness defects; it does **not** establish that the jobs process inputs and produce outputs exactly as intended.

The recurring failures are false-success/coverage claims, incompatible producer-consumer contracts, incorrect source/graph identities, stale or overwritten evidence, and incomplete handling of bounds or errors. Four distinct unit-test failures were reported and remain unfixed; their agents attribute them to stale fixtures/assertions. No repository code was changed, and no live review was executed.
