`scripts/scancode-toolkit-src/` is treated as vendored third-party source. Completed historical

The destination rules are:

- deterministic legacy engagement transforms go to `pipeline/`;
- accepted evidence producers become bounded, run-owned jobs under `appsec-review-process/`;
- container payloads and image builders go to an image/tooling-owned area;
- local development and operator helpers move only after the owner selects a durable home;
- superseded artifacts are deleted after callers and tracked documentation are updated.

## Batch selection notes

The static prepass remains the highest-value migration, but it is not one small job: it mixes
source SAST, secrets, IaC/container checks, SBOM/SCA, binary hardening, mobile scanning, indexing,
summarization and scrubbing. It must be decomposed, not copied. The first small batch should
therefore be one independently contractible tool producer or one deterministic transform with all
of its callers updated. An implemented child producer does not make the aggregate
`02-source-sast` node complete until every required source-SAST producer and the aggregate
acceptance policy are defined.
